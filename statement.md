Smart Study Analyzer

1. Problem Statement

Students often find it difficult to analyse their study habits and understand whether they are using their study time effectively. They may study for different amounts of time each day but may not calculate their average study hours, most productive day, or overall study performance.

The Smart Study Analyzer is a Python-based project designed to analyse a student's study hours and provide simple and useful information about their study pattern.

2. Target Users

The main target users are students who want to track and analyse their study habits.

3. Scope

The project will take the student's daily study hours as input and analyse the data using Python and NumPy. It will calculate total study hours, average study hours, maximum and minimum study hours, identify the most productive day, and classify overall study performance.

4. Main Features

- Enter student name and number of study days
- Enter daily study hours
- Calculate total study hours
- Calculate average study hours
- Find maximum and minimum study hours
- Identify the most productive day
- Classify study performance
- Display the analysis in a simple format

5. Technologies Used

- Python
- NumPy

6. Expected Outcome

The project will help students understand their study patterns through simple numerical analysis and encourage them to manage their study time more effectively.
